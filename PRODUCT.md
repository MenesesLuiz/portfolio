# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, and Motion. Deployment target is undecided.

## Users

The primary audience is not yet confirmed. Future work must support fast evaluation by the intended professional audience without assuming whether that is recruiting, client work, or partnerships.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents work and professional capability across Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, and Web Development.

## Positioning

The portfolio brings together software, security, and web work in one evidence-led professional presence rather than treating those disciplines as separate profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A single portfolio surface will include profile context, certifications, technical stack, websites, engineering and cybersecurity projects, and professional contact links.
- The implementation must use a validated 3D Tilt Card component from the 21st.dev MCP for the certifications section.
- The build approach is code-first.
- Personal identity, professional links, certification details, project details, site URLs, and media assets are undecided and must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

- The experience should feel technological, clean, and high quality.
- Avoid generic AI styling, including purple or blue gradients and excessive nested card layouts.

## Evidence on Hand

No portfolio copy, project records, certificate images, professional links, or personal imagery are currently available in the repository.

## Product Principles

- Show real work and verifiable credentials rather than invented claims.
- Make multidisciplinary capability easy to scan and understand.
- Keep interactions performant, purposeful, and accessible.
- Give each discipline a coherent place without fragmenting the portfolio.
